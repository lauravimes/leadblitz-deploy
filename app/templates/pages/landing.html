FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is LeadBlitz?

LeadBlitz is an AI-powered lead generation tool built for freelance web designers and small agencies. It helps you find local businesses with outdated websites, scores their sites using AI, and generates professional audit reports you can send to win new clients.

How does LeadBlitz find leads?

LeadBlitz searches Google Places to discover local businesses in your target area and industry. It then scores signals in fetched website content on a scale of 0–100. Higher scores mean stronger detected website signals, not greater buying intent. Check the live website before using findings in outreach.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every new account gets 200 free credits with no credit card required. That's enough to search for leads, score websites, and generate audit reports so you can see the full value of the platform before purchasing additional credits.

What does a website audit report include?

Reports summarise detected technical and content signals, with AI suggestions for improvement. They do not replace browser testing of speed, mobile layout or forms. Review each claim before sharing a branded PDF with a potential client.

Who is LeadBlitz designed for?

LeadBlitz is built for freelance web designers, small web design agencies, and digital marketing consultants who do outbound prospecting. If you sell website design, redesign, or maintenance services to local businesses, LeadBlitz helps you find and convert the right prospects.
